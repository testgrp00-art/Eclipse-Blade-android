SETTINGS

Survival mode: You have 300 HP for the entire run. Enemy attacks reduce health; at 0 HP you die and must restart. Unlimited levels and enemies continue until death. No fight timer or visible cooldown seconds. Best performance: Balanced for most phones, High for powerful phones and tablets.

ABOUT THE GAME

ECLIPSE BLADE
Original 2D dark fantasy action game. Three heroes, 300 HP survival mode with permanent death at 0 HP, endlessly paced levels until death, recurring boss battles every fourth level, shadow enemies and combos. High-resolution artwork and fast continuous combat.
